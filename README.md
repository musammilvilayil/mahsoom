2. Confirm the exact street/landmark or Google Business Profile URL for the centre.
3. Add club timings and any final programme/pricing details supplied by the owner.
4. Keep health/product statements aligned with current Herbalife India labels and official guidance.
5. Do not publish guaranteed weight-loss, disease-treatment or fabricated testimonial claims.
